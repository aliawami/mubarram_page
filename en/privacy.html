Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

At Mubarram, we take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and what we never do with it.

1. Data We Collect

Data Where it's stored Purpose
Full name Our servers (Supabase) Account identification, appears on generated PDFs
Email address Our servers (Supabase) Sign-in and password reset
Tax number (optional) Our servers (Supabase) Generating tax-ready invoices with a tax QR code
Client data (name, phone, email, company) Our servers (Supabase) Linking contracts and invoices to clients
Contracts and invoices Our servers (Supabase) Record-keeping and PDF generation
Profile logo (optional) Our servers (Supabase Storage) Included in generated PDFs
IBAN (optional) Your device only (encrypted storage) Appears on PDFs — never sent to our servers
The IBAN is an important exception: it's stored locally on your device only, using secure encryption, and never reaches our servers. Deleting the app or changing devices means it's permanently lost, since we keep no copy of it.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data exclusively to provide the app's services:

— Enabling you to create and manage contracts and invoices

— Generating PDF files that carry your data

— Securing your account through the authentication system

— Sending password reset emails on request

3. What We Never Do

We don't sell your data. We don't share it with third parties for marketing purposes. We don't use it to build advertising profiles. We don't send marketing messages without your permission.

4. Service Provider (Supabase)

Your data stored on our servers is hosted by Supabase, a cloud database platform. Supabase adheres to high security standards and provides encryption for data in transit and at rest.

You can review Supabase's privacy policy at: supabase.com/privacy

5. Data Security

We apply database-level protection mechanisms (Row Level Security) that ensure each user can only see and access their own data. No user can view another user's data.

All communication between the app and the server is encrypted via HTTPS.

6. Data Retention Period

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, all your data is deleted from our servers within 30 days.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

— Access the data we hold about you

— Correct any inaccurate data

— Delete your account and all your data

— Export your data (contracts and invoices as PDF files)

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at: support@mubrram.app

8. Children's Privacy

The app is intended for adult independent professionals. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under the age of 18.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as needed. We will notify you of any material changes via an in-app notice. The last-updated date is shown at the top of this page.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about your privacy or your data, we'd love to hear from you at: support@mubrram.app
