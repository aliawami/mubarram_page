Delete Account and Data

You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time.

⚠️ Warning — This Action Cannot Be Undone

  • All your contracts, invoices, and client data will be permanently deleted
  • You will not be able to recover any data after the request is processed
  • Any active subscription will be canceled, and paid fees will not be refunded

Method 1 — Instant Deletion

Sign in with your Mubarram account to delete your account and all your data immediately.

✅ Your Account Has Been Deleted

Your account and all your data have been successfully deleted. We wish you the best.

Method 2 — Via Email

If you're unable to sign in, you can submit the request by email instead.

You don't need to keep the app installed on your device to submit this request.

Send an email to our support team from the email address linked to your account, and include in your message:

1. The email address linked to your account
2. Subject line: Account Deletion Request — Mubarram

Send Deletion Request

Processing Time

Method 1 (sign-in) is instant — your account is deleted as soon as you confirm.

Method 2 (via email) is processed within 7 business days of receiving the request. You'll receive a confirmation email once the deletion is complete.

Data That Gets Deleted

When the request is processed, all of the following data is deleted completely and permanently:

User account and sign-in data · Contracts and invoices · Client data · Account and profile settings
