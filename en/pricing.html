Frequently Asked Questions

Why is there a client limit on the free plan, but not a document limit?

Because billing an existing client is normal, healthy use of the app — not something that should ever cost you. The limit is on how many different clients you're actively working with at once, not on how many quotations, contracts, or invoices you send any one of them. Close out a client you're done working with (archive them) and that slot opens back up.

What's the Mubrram mark?

A small logo and a one-line credit at the bottom of your generated PDF. It's there by default on the free plan; the paid plan removes it.

Can I create documents in Arabic?

Yes — Mubrram lets you write contracts and invoices fully in Arabic, alongside other languages, wherever your clients are.

Is the contract template legal advice?

No. It's a real, signable contract template — not a document reviewed by a lawyer for your specific deal. Treat it as solid paperwork hygiene, not a substitute for legal counsel if your situation calls for it.
