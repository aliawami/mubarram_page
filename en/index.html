Features

Everything you need for your next contract

Clear contracts

Create professional contracts, send them to your client, and track their status from draft to completion.

A real, signable contract template — not a legal review of your specific case or legal advice.

Instant invoices

Generate the invoice directly from the contract, without re-entering any data.

Professional, tax-ready formatting on every invoice, including an automatic QR code once you activate your tax number.

Client management

Native Arabic PDF

Contracts and invoices in fully-formatted Arabic PDF, ready to share instantly.

Why Mubarram?

Most freelancers manage their contracts and invoices over WhatsApp or scattered Word files — no system, no real protection.

I built Mubarram myself, as a freelancer who lived this chaos every day. Mubarram — meaning "sealed, concluded, tied" — is a small, single-purpose tool: it turns your verbal agreement into a document you can respect, in minutes.
